MYKARR – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

MYKARR keeps track of your car: services, fuel, reminders, tickets and builds. This policy explains, in plain words, what data the app uses, why, and what you can do about it. You can use MYKARR without an account, and then everything stays on your phone.

Who we are

MYKARR is made by Ralfs Karro, Drustu Iela 18A, Mārupe, Latvia ("we", "us"). We are the data controller for the data described here.

Questions or requests about your data: app.mykarr@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days, usually much sooner.

What data MYKARR uses

DataExamplesWhere it is kept

We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and we do not collect your location, contacts or other data from your phone.

Scanning. When you scan a receipt, an invoice or a part, the photo or PDF is sent to Google's Gemini AI so it can be read. Only the text it finds comes back to the app. The original stays with your record, like any other photo you attach.

Notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your phone. No notification service on our side is involved.

Why we use it

We use your data only to run MYKARR for you. We never sell it, and we don't use it for advertising.

We may also disclose data if the law requires it, for example to a court or the police with a valid legal request.

Where it is stored and for how long

Our database, file storage and sync service are in the European Union. Google may process a scanned photo briefly on its servers in other countries; that transfer is covered by Google's data processing terms and the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

DataHow long we keep it
Account, profile, car data, photos and filesUntil you delete them or delete your account
Scan usageUntil you delete your account
Server logsUp to 90 days
Data on your phoneUntil you delete it or uninstall the app

When you delete your account, its online data is deleted within 30 days, including backups held by our providers.

Data is encrypted on its way between your phone and our servers. Access to the database is locked per account, so you only ever see your own cars.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can:

  • See the data we hold about you and get a copy
  • Correct anything that is wrong (most of it you can edit in the app)
  • Delete your data or your whole account
  • Take it with you: export everything in Settings → Backup & restore
  • Object to or limit how we use it

Deleting your account. Go to Settings → Account → Delete account. This removes your account and all its online data. You can also email app.mykarr@gmail.com from your account's address and we'll do it for you. More about deleting your account.

If you think we've handled your data wrongly, please tell us first so we can fix it. You can also complain to the Latvian Data State Inspectorate (Datu valsts inspekcija) or the data protection authority where you live.

Age

You must be at least 18 to create a MYKARR account or use scanning. When you sign up, we ask for your date of birth to confirm this. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change matters, for example a new service that handles your data, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect.

MYKARR keeps growing. We plan to add features such as tutorials, a marketplace, Q&A and membership tiers. These may use new kinds of data, for example listings or posts that other users can see, or payments handled by a payment provider. This policy will change as they arrive. We'll update it before each feature launches and tell you in the app, so you always know what's new before you use it.
