Using MYKARR fairly

Please don't:

If an account breaks these rules, we may limit or close it. Where we can, we'll tell you first and give you a chance to explain.

Scanning

  • Signed-in users get a free number of scans each month (currently 30). The app always shows how many you have left. We may change the number; if it goes down, we'll say so in the app first.
  • Scans are read by AI and can be wrong: a swapped number, a misread date, the wrong part size. Always check the filled-in form before you save it.
  • Only scan things that are yours or that you're allowed to share, like your own receipts, invoices and car parts.
